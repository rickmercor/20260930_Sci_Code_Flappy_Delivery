## Background

An electric field changes the charge distribution and energy of a molecular system. In charge equilibration, charges minimize a quadratic energy under a fixed total-charge constraint. Differentiating the relaxed energy gives a polarizability tensor that includes charge redistribution and explicit field curvature.

Environmental fluctuations produce correlated changes in the molecular polarizability. A polarization projection gives a scalar time series; its autocorrelation integral measures the magnitude and duration of the response fluctuations. Low-frequency spectral fitting estimates this integral from finite trajectories. Correlated-window cross-validation and Lorentz parameter uncertainties determine how different frequency cutoffs contribute to the estimate.
